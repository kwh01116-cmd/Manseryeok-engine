# Experimental analysis policy reference

Only the optional experimental weighted-support policy refers to the arithmetic
design of `glee1228/korean_saju`, revision
`8c81256db10d3179d31f551beacaa69f2ab2fcc5`,
`src/korean_saju/saju/ilgan_strength.py` (version 0.3.0).
The adaptation uses this repository's existing M2 tables and deliberately excludes
upstream automatic stem transformation, missing-hour imputation and the extra
亥 hidden 戊 member. It is not a verbatim equivalent of the upstream policy.
