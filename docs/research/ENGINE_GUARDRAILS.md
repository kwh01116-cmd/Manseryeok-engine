# Engine guardrails

## Current scope

The first implementation phase only promotes rules whose semantics are stable enough to test without choosing a disputed school policy. The deterministic foundation includes the ten heavenly stems, twelve earthly branches, five-element and yin/yang classifications, the 60-cycle, and stem-to-stem ten-god relations.

Astronomical boundaries, hidden stems, seasonal strength, combinations/transformation, day rollover at Zi, true-solar-time usage, strength scoring, useful-god selection, pattern formation, and luck interpretation remain separate workstreams until their rule provenance and policies are explicit.

## Korea-first source hierarchy

For Korean calendar/time facts, prefer:

1. Korea AeroSpace Administration / official gazette or official calendar notices when applicable.
2. Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute almanac or computational references.
3. Independent astronomical calculation used as a cross-check.
4. Myeongli libraries only as implementation comparisons, never as the calendar authority.

## Stability principle

A later research result should be able to replace an interpretation resolver without changing already-verified calendar facts. Conversely, a corrected historical-time or astronomical boundary result must invalidate downstream pillars explicitly rather than being hidden by interpretation code.
